Clinical & Procedural Validity

Peter DeBlieux, MD

LSU Health New Orleans · Assistant Dean for Advanced Learning and Simulation

Biography

Dr. Peter DeBlieux is a pulmonary, critical care, and emergency physician with decades of practice in a Level One Trauma Center. His career combines frontline clinical care with medical education, including ten years directing an emergency medicine residency and extensive work in simulation and procedural education. He serves as Assistant Dean for Advanced Learning and Simulation at LSU Health New Orleans.

LinkedIn Email

Core assignment in COPES

Ensures clinical and procedural validity across the platform. He validates scenario content, coaching behavior, procedural workflows, and performance standards so that COPES reflects realistic clinical practice.

Instructional Design & Analytics

Kevin Lord, PhD, MHS, RRT

LSU Health New Orleans · Assistant Dean of Student Affairs & Associate Professor

Biography

Dr. Kevin Lord is an educator and researcher whose work spans cardiopulmonary science, pharmacology, clinical physiology, curriculum development, and educational technology. His research includes the use of haptic feedback, virtual and augmented reality, and gamification for foundational clinical-skills training, together with experience in curriculum blueprinting and learner remediation.

LinkedIn Email

Core assignment in COPES

Directs the instructional architecture of COPES, including competency mapping, curriculum blueprinting, learner progression, and the integration of coaching and performance analytics into the training experience.

Healthcare Simulation & Implementation

Adam Dodson, MBA, NRP, NCEE, CCEMTP

Healthcare Simulation & Clinical Education · Johns Hopkins / Laerdal Medical

Biography

Adam Dodson is a healthcare educator, clinician, author, and simulation specialist with more than 30 years of experience in emergency medicine, resuscitation, airway management, human factors, and clinical education. His background includes leadership in the Johns Hopkins Medicine Simulation Center and work in simulation strategy, curriculum development, educational data science, systems integration, and outcomes-focused implementation.

LinkedIn Email

Core assignment in COPES

Advises simulation and AI integration, curriculum design, fidelity monitoring, performance measurement, dashboards, and implementation strategy, helping translate COPES into a rigorous and deployable clinical-training system.

Platform Architecture & Technical Development

Felipe Fronchetti, PhD

Louisiana State University · Assistant Professor of Computer Science · RISE Lab PI

Biography

Dr. Felipe Fronchetti is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Louisiana State University and principal investigator of the Robotics and Software Engineering Laboratory. His research spans software engineering, human-computer interaction, robotics, virtual reality, and intelligent interactive systems, with an emphasis on translating emerging technologies into usable human-centered applications.

LinkedIn Email

Core assignment in COPES

Leads COPES technical development, including VR platform architecture, AI–VR integration, performance capture and analytics, software engineering, and the coordination of iterative development and usability evaluation.
